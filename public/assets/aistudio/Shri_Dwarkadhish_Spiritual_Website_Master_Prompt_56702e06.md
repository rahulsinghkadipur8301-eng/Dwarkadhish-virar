## 1. Role, outcome and non-negotiable priorities

Act as a senior creative director, Indian cultural experience designer, Next.js engineer, accessibility specialist and cinematic motion designer. Design and build a complete, polished, responsive spiritual website for **Shri Dwarkadhish Mandir, Shirgaon, Virar East, Palghar, Maharashtra, India**, using the project brief as the starting point for its identity and locality.

The website must feel like entering a serene Indian temple courtyard: warm cream stone, light brown carvings, soft morning light, devotional detail and a continuous sense of Shri Krishna's presence. Make it highly animated and visually memorable while keeping visitor information easy to find and read.

Build the complete experience, including interior pages and working demonstration interactions. A beautiful hero followed by generic cards is insufficient.

Use this priority order when requirements compete:

1. Correct temple identity and accurate visitor information, especially location.
2. A clear, sustained Shri Dwarkadhish and Krishna devotional identity.
3. Cream and light brown art direction across the entire public website.
4. Distinctive spiritual animation and scroll storytelling.
5. Working navigation, multilingual content and useful visitor tools.
6. Mobile usability, accessibility, performance and maintainability.

**Project status: Independent Concept Demonstration — Not Official.** Preserve this status in a slim sitewide notice and the footer. Demonstrate proposed services honestly; activate official services only when their content and integrations are authorized.

This project concerns the temple in **Shirgaon, Virar**, not the Dwarkadhish Temple in Dwarka, Gujarat, or any other similarly named temple. Do not import another temple's history, deity photographs, address, aarti schedule, management, architecture or visitor policies.
